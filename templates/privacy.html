Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain your personal expense tracking experience.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Display spending summaries, breakdowns, and history within the app.
  • Communicate service updates or important account notices.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database accessible only to you and authorised system processes. Passwords are never stored in plain text — we use industry-standard hashing before any credential is saved. You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We use Google Fonts to load typefaces — this results in a request to Google's servers when you visit the app. No expense or account data is transmitted to any external service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out through the app's support channel. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
